Popular GST slabs

  • 3% — selected gold and jewellery items
  • 5% — essentials, rail tickets, packaged foods
  • 12% — business class services and processed goods
  • 18% — most products and professional services
  • 28% — luxury goods and premium products

Formula guide

  • Add GST: Amount × GST rate ÷ 100
  • Total with GST: Base amount + GST amount
  • Extract GST: Inclusive amount × rate ÷ (100 + rate)
  • CGST / SGST split: GST amount ÷ 2
